# Anatolia

Focus region: southeastern Anatolia and Upper Mesopotamia. That means Göbekli Tepe, Karahan Tepe, the wider Taş Tepeler landscape, the Harran plain, and the Tektek and Germuş limestone plateaus.

The project is now a world atlas of published sites. This region stays its deep end: the known-site validation area, with its own terrain atlas, place guides, registers and reading lists. The world side is described in the [root README](../README.md) and [docs/world-atlas.md](../docs/world-atlas.md).

## Current phase

Data gathering, before a direction is chosen. Sources, licences, coverage, and what is still unverified are in [data-sources.md](data-sources.md). A local 3D view of the published sites on Copernicus DEM and one Sentinel-2 day is in [explorer/](explorer/README.md). The web app is in [atlas/](atlas/README.md). It opens on the world globe, and the Turkey terrain atlas is one of its three views. Raw rasters stay out of git.
GitHub repositories and X accounts from the same pass are in [github-and-x.md](github-and-x.md).

The atlas now has place guides, historical map links and a Landscape research panel. [Educational sources](educational-sources.md) brings together field reports, museum collections, talks and reading questions for the Şanlıurfa sites. [Historical maps and landscape research](maps-and-landscape-research.md) covers archive sheets, environmental layers, survey coverage and a plan for testing landscape models.

## Candidate directions

These are open options. None of them is a detection result.

1. **CORONA-based mapping of mounds, hollow ways, and field systems** on the Harran plain and the Tigris lowlands, following Menze & Ur 2012, Orengo et al. 2020, and Pistola et al. 2025.
2. **Visual and stereo CORONA reading** for quarry hollows and enclosure-scale marks on the limestone plateaus, checked against published sites only.
3. **Landscape-context modelling** with 30 m DEMs, HydroSHEDS, and SoilGrids.

## Validation sites

Göbekli Tepe and Karahan Tepe are known, published sites. They are for validation and method-testing. They are not discoveries of this project.

Do not add coordinates of unpublished or candidate sites to this folder. The register records published sources and, where a famous site is already in an official listing, only what that listing already states. The world catalogue under `atlas/public/world/` follows the same rule at a larger scale: published records from registered open sources only. See the Ethics section of the [root README](../README.md).
