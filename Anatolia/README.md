# Anatolia

Focus region: southeastern Anatolia and Upper Mesopotamia. That means Göbekli Tepe, Karahan Tepe, the wider Taş Tepeler landscape, the Harran plain, and the Tektek and Germuş limestone plateaus.

## Current phase

Data gathering, before a direction is chosen. Sources, licences, coverage, and what is still unverified are in [data-sources.md](data-sources.md). A local 3D view of the published sites on Copernicus DEM and one Sentinel-2 day is in [explorer/](explorer/README.md). The wider Turkey atlas is in [atlas/](atlas/README.md). Raw rasters stay out of git.
GitHub repositories and X accounts from the same pass are in [github-and-x.md](github-and-x.md).

## Candidate directions

These are open options. None of them is a detection result.

1. **CORONA-based mapping of mounds, hollow ways, and field systems** on the Harran plain and the Tigris lowlands, following Menze & Ur 2012, Orengo et al. 2020, and Pistola et al. 2025.
2. **Visual and stereo CORONA reading** for quarry hollows and enclosure-scale marks on the limestone plateaus, checked against published sites only.
3. **Landscape-context modelling** with 30 m DEMs, HydroSHEDS, and SoilGrids.

## Validation sites

Göbekli Tepe and Karahan Tepe are known, published sites. They are for validation and method-testing. They are not discoveries of this project.

Do not add coordinates of unpublished or candidate sites to this folder. The register records published sources and, where a famous site is already in an official listing, only what that listing already states.
