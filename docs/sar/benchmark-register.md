# Published SAR benchmark register

This register is a shortlist for eligibility review. Only the published Giza pyramid fields have a catalogue search ID. Held-out sites, negative controls and eligibility thresholds are pending.

| Published target | Proposed role | Evidence and outstanding checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Khufu pyramid, Egypt | Non-blind calibration for known chambers and passages | Separate above-grade masonry from bedrock by datum. Confirm diagram or survey scale, uncertainty and reuse terms before scoring. Search ID `khufu` covers the published pyramid fields with a coarse box. [UNESCO listing and maps](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/86/maps/) · [ScanPyramids paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature24647) |
| Publicly surveyed limestone show caves | Potential shallow held-out stratum | Select a documented cave survey with permission to use the geometry and evidence for ranking surveyed negative volumes. |
| CERN Large Hadron Collider, France/Switzerland | Potential intermediate-depth held-out stratum | Check usable public survey geometry, depth reference and observation conditions. [Civil engineering overview](https://www.ice.org.uk/what-is-civil-engineering/infrastructure-projects/civil-engineering-at-cern) |
| Gran Sasso National Laboratory, Italy | Potential deep held-out stratum under mountain cover | Check public geometry and overburden locally. Control for steep slopes and differentiate physical cover from metres water equivalent. [INFN overview](https://www.lngs.infn.it/en/lngs-overview/) |
| Boulby Underground Laboratory, UK | Potential deep held-out stratum | Test eligibility against public geometry and physical depth. A facility depth alone gives no object boundaries for scoring. [Laboratory overview](https://www.boulby.stfc.ac.uk/about/) |

For each scored benchmark, record the ground-truth citation, licence, geometry representation, physical depth and datum, rock type, dimensions, survey uncertainty, scene availability and survey limits. For a negative control, require independently supported absence of comparable voids within a stated volume and report the survey's detection limits. Areas of unknown geology remain unlabelled.

Design strata are inside a standing monument, 0–30 m, 30–100 m, 100–300 m, 300–650 m and deeper than 650 m. They are bins for reporting coverage and outcomes. Depth-stratified comparisons require enough independent sites and matched controls per stratum and should report gaps. Use only publicly documented geometry and keep precise site outputs local.
