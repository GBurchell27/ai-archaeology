# Method and evidence audit

The original journal article was retracted on 10 August 2026. The notice reports serious methodological flaws and statistical errors without itemizing them. The article remains a description of the method to test, and its reported structures carry no validation weight in this study. [Original journal article](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs14205231) · [arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.00811) · [Retraction](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs18162679)

## From focused data to the proposed inversion

The references below use section and equation numbers from the journal version. The paper's external interferometric examples use paired acquisitions in §5.1. The experiment in this repository concerns the single-SLC interior workflow in §4, Figure 9.

| Operation | What the paper specifies | What must be resolved before implementation or interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focused input | §§3.1–3.2, Eqs. (1)–(3) and (6) model a point response in range and azimuth and express it in the spectral domain. Table 1 gives 9.6 GHz carrier, 400 MHz chirp bandwidth, 22 kHz Doppler bandwidth and 15 s acquisition duration for COSMO-SkyMed spotlight. Table 2 lists dates, beams and polarisations. | Obtain product IDs, orbit and timing annotations, and the exact processing history. Table 1 lists 2.0 kHz PRF and 0.23 ms PRT, which are not reciprocals as quoted. Establish which sampling stage each figure describes. |
| Spectral sublooks | §3.1, Eqs. (4)–(5) and Figure 7 describe paired master/slave Doppler bands stepped across the azimuth spectrum. The description sets the unprocessed width to half the total Doppler bandwidth. §4, Figure 9 includes bandpass filters and selection of pixels along a tomographic line. | Record actual band edges, step size, overlap, window shapes, alignment and transform conventions. Derive the effective time response and correlation between looks from the acquisition and processing. A focused SLC does not provide independent instantaneous time-series samples at the radar pulse rate. |
| Motion proxy | §3.2, Eqs. (7)–(12) relate range history to range velocity, range acceleration and azimuth velocity. §4, Figure 9 describes estimating shifts by pixel tracking. Eqs. (13)–(21) turn those estimates into an oscillator-based vector for the inversion. | Establish the registration window, interpolator, rejection mask, scale and units of tracked shifts, and the effect of static geometry and speckle. Validate phase and pixel-shift observables separately. The oscillator, damping and forcing are model assumptions. |
| Depth response | §4, Eqs. (22)–(24) use a baseline-dependent steering matrix and pseudoinverse. The surrounding text relates resolution to acoustic wavelength and aperture and illustrates a chosen investigation frequency. | Resolve radar versus acoustic wavelength, the definition and units of the depth/time product in Eq. (22), and the frequency and factor-of-two convention in the numerical example. Record depth datum, masks, normalisation, regularisation and singular-value cutoff. A displayed peak has a physical depth only if the conversion is independently calibrated and identifiable. |

The paper's data-availability statement provides no usable archive. Its acknowledgments mention SARPROZ and the Italian Space Agency data. A reproducibility request would seek product identifiers and permissible access, exact sub-band and tracking settings, software/version and intermediate arrays, and the steering matrix and depth conversion used for published figures.

## External work

- Pietro Canini describes a Capella-based reconstruction on published Khufu chambers. The test uses the King's Chamber for calibration and previously inspected scenes. Its archive omits some Capella readers. The report also documents a withdrawn comparison of unlike quantities and a finer estimator that changes the prevalence of zero shifts. The page offers code and a sealed test protocol, but gives no evidence here of a complete, licensed, executable test suite. Treat this as an external reconstruction. [Accadrà di nuovo Lab report and files](https://www.accadradinuovo.com/Lab/giza_sar_verifica_en.html)
- Hassan Foreman's v6.3 preprint reports control runs on commercial X-band open data, tests of the depth-axis scaling and a no-recovery result under its registered decision rule. Its public repository declares MIT for its code and links separate data terms. Preregistration, code and controls improve traceability. The units, numerical correctness, test coverage, deviations from registration and sensitivity to assumed wavelength remain to be independently checked before adopting implementation details. [Preprint](https://www.academia.edu/169418222/No_Reproducible_Evidence_for_Deep_Subsurface_Structures_Beneath_the_Giza_Plateau_A_Pre_Registered_Reproduction_of_Single_Pass_SAR_Doppler_Micro_Motion_Tomography_and_an_Audit_of_Its_Published_Imagery_v6_3_November_2026_) · [Source repository](https://github.com/Hassanforeman/subsurface-sar-tomo)

No third-party implementation was imported. Sentinel-1 C-band TOPS uses different timing, focusing and imaging geometry from the paper's X-band spotlight products. It can help bound feasibility in that acquisition regime. Matching the original acquisition and method requires the original products and processing information. [Sentinel-1 processing documentation](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s1-processing)
